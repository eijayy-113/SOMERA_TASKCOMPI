HERE WHEN YOU NEED US

Help & privacy

A few clear answers about this little money tool.

01

GETTING STARTED

How does Somera work?

Add expenses by hand, choose a category, and set monthly limits to help you see your spending in one place. The totals and category breakdown update as you go.

Log an expense →
02

YOUR PRIVACY

Where does my data go?

This demo stores entries in your browser's local storage. It doesn't connect to a bank, use an account login, or transmit your personal details to a server.

● No bank connection. No account required.

03

YOUR CONTROL

Can I clear my demo data?

Yes. Clearing data removes your locally saved expenses and resets budget limits to the starting examples. This action can't be undone.

04

A FRIENDLY REMINDER

Is this financial advice?

No. Somera is an educational prototype to help organize example expenses. It isn't a bank, financial adviser, or substitute for professional guidance.
